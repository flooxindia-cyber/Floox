✨ Our Story

India ka Live Music Hub.

India has some of the world's most extraordinary musicians, vocalists, and performers — yet most of them go undiscovered, and most events go unheard of. Floox changes that.

We're a platform that connects talented artists directly with event organisers — and the result? More live events, better performances, and richer experiences for everyone who loves music.

"Every great performance starts with a connection. Floox is where that connection begins."

As a fan, Floox lets you discover upcoming events in your city, follow your favourite artists, and be the first to know when something special is happening near you.
