Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Floox Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Note: Floox is an early-stage platform. This policy reflects our current data practices. We will update it as our product and services grow. By using Floox, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Floox is an online marketplace that connects live music artists with event organisers and fans across India. We are operated by Floox Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Floox", "we", "our", "us"). Our platform is accessible at www.floox.in.

For privacy-related queries, contact us at: hello@floox.in

2. Information We Collect

When you use Floox, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, city, and password (stored as a hashed value — we never store plain-text passwords).
  • Profile information: Stage name, bio, genres, performance history, profile photo, fee range, and other details you choose to share on your public profile.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, searches performed, events viewed, and actions taken on the platform (e.g., likes, follows, bookings).
  • Communications: Messages sent through the platform's messaging system between artists and organisers.
  • Device and technical data: Browser type, device type, IP address, and referring URL — collected automatically through standard web server logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to:

  • Create and manage your Floox account.
  • Display your public artist or organiser profile to other users.
  • Facilitate connections and enquiries between artists and event organisers.
  • Send you transactional emails (account verification, password reset, booking notifications).
  • Improve the platform — understanding how people use Floox helps us build a better product.
  • Comply with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

We do not use your data to show third-party advertisements, and we do not sell your personal information to any third party.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only in the following limited ways:

  • Other Floox users: Your public profile information (name, city, genres, photo, bio) is visible to other registered users. Private contact details are only shared with users you have accepted a connection or booking enquiry from.
  • Service providers: We use Supabase (database), Vercel (hosting), and Cloudinary (image storage) to run the platform. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by a court order, government authority, or applicable law in India.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with marketing companies or data brokers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion (see Section 7), we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g., for tax or fraud-prevention purposes).

Anonymised, aggregated analytics data (e.g., total search queries by city) may be retained indefinitely as it cannot be linked to any individual.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your personal data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — we cannot recover your plain-text password.
  • Access to the production database is restricted to authorised team members only.
  • JWT tokens used for authentication expire and are not stored server-side.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at hello@floox.in.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw consent: Where we rely on your consent to process data, you may withdraw it at any time (this will not affect processing done before withdrawal).
  • Grievance redressal: Lodge a complaint with our grievance officer (contact below).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@floox.in with the subject line "Privacy Request". We will respond within 72 hours.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Floox uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your authentication token and basic session information on your device. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to third parties. Clearing your browser storage will log you out of Floox.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

9. Children's Privacy

Floox is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete their account. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us at hello@floox.in.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top and, for significant changes, notify registered users via email. Continued use of Floox after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact & Grievance Officer

For any questions, complaints, or data requests relating to this Privacy Policy:

  • Email: hello@floox.in
  • Platform: floox.in/contact

We aim to resolve all privacy-related queries within 72 hours of receipt.
